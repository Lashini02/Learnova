# Learnova Student Learning & Task Organizer

## Project Title

**Learnova – Student Learning & Task Organizer** 

---

## Project Overview

Learnova is a mobile application designed to help students organize their academic activities effectively. The app focuses on improving time management, assignment tracking, study planning, and academic productivity. 

The project supports **Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 4 – Quality Education**, aiming to provide equal and accessible learning support for students. 

---

## Problem Statement

Many students struggle with:

* Managing assignments and deadlines
* Organizing study schedules
* Tracking academic progress
* Handling multiple courses and projects

These issues can lead to stress, missed submissions, and poor academic performance. 

---

## Proposed Solution

Learnova provides:

* Course management
* Assignment and task tracking
* Study planning
* Progress monitoring
* Reminder notifications
* User profile management

The application is designed with a simple and student-friendly interface to improve productivity and academic organization. 

---

## Target Users

* College students
* University students
* Professional course students
* Students needing better study organization 

---
